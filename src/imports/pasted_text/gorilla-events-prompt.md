**Prompt:**

Design and build a **modern, premium, responsive marketing website** for **Gorilla Events**, a leading event production and management company based in Rwanda. Use the visual style, layout inspiration, spacing, typography, component patterns, and design language from the provided Figma reference while adapting it to Gorilla Events' brand and services. The final design should feel cinematic, high-end, professional, and suitable for large-scale concerts, conferences, corporate events, festivals, weddings, and live productions.

### Brand Positioning

Gorilla Events transforms ideas into unforgettable experiences through world-class event production, combining creativity, innovation, and technical excellence.

### Primary Slogan

**Your Vision. Our Production.**

### Secondary Tagline

**Planning. Production. Perfection.**

# Overall Design Direction

Create a premium experience with:

* Dark luxury aesthetic
* Black, charcoal, and deep gray backgrounds
* White typography with subtle gray secondary text
* Gold or warm orange accent color inspired by professional stage lighting
* Large imagery and cinematic event photography
* Smooth rounded cards
* Glassmorphism where appropriate
* Soft shadows
* Premium gradients
* Modern icons
* Plenty of whitespace
* Micro interactions
* Hover animations
* Smooth scrolling
* Large typography
* Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
* Accessibility-focused color contrast

The overall feeling should resemble leading event production companies rather than a simple equipment rental business.
